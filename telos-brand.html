Contrast law (both reviewers): bronze-on-ink ≈ 7:1 — the hero pairing. Never set bronze text on bone (≈2.2:1, fails). Bronze is the one must-keep both heads agreed on — the Talos story in one hex code.

Open question — the sound problem (Opus, uncomfortable truth)

Spoken aloud, "T-ELOS" is "TELOS" — and Australian deceptive-similarity law weighs sound heavily. Punctuation is near-invisible to an examiner. The device improves the odds; it does not guarantee registration while a rival owns the bare word in class 9. Before artwork spend: a clearance opinion (~$300–600) and a hard look at adding a distinguishing word element to the name. The register gave us the company; the trademark still has to be earned.
